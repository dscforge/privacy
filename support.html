Before you email

Most issues are resolved by one of these. Please try them first — it gets you an answer faster than waiting on a reply.

  • Check the column header. Pasted CSV is expected to start with Subject,Start Date,End Date,Category. A different header order is the usual cause of events landing on the wrong day or being skipped.
  • Importing replaces the list, it does not add to it. Importing a file takes the place of what was there. A file that has no events, or whose rows do not parse, is rejected before anything is written, so a bad file will not wipe a good list.
  • The grid always starts the week on Sunday. This is fixed and is not a setting, so a grid that looks shifted by one column is behaving as designed rather than being misconfigured. Settings covers colour palette and light or dark appearance only.
  • Choose your export format deliberately. PDF — Full puts one month per page, PDF — Condensed packs the range into a compact multi-month sheet, and PNG is a single image, which is the one to drop straight into a slide.

Helping us reproduce a bug

If something is broken, these four details are what we actually need:

  • Which device and iOS version.
  • The exact steps from launch to the problem.
  • What you expected, versus what happened.
  • A screenshot, and if it involves an import, a two-row sample of the input.
  • Whether you imported CSV or an .ics file — the two paths are parsed differently.

On privacy, briefly

The apps collect nothing and contain no networking code, so there is no account to reset, no server log to search, and no data for us to look up on your behalf. That is also why support is email rather than an in-app chat: there is no connection for a chat to travel over. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
