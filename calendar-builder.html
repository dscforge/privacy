Privacy Policy

Calendar Builder · Effective 2 November 2026
Published by DSC Forge LLC

The short version: Calendar Builder has no network code at all. It never contacts a server, so it cannot collect, upload, sell, or share your data. Everything you enter stays inside the app's own private storage on your device, and it leaves your device only if you explicitly export it yourself.

What the app handles

Calendar Builder is a utility that renders a printable calendar from events you give it. The data it touches is data you put into it:

  • Event titles, dates, and categories you type in.
  • Calendar text you paste in or import yourself (.ics and raw CSV).
  • Your appearance choices — palette and layout options.

Where that data lives

  • In the app's own sandboxed storage on your device. iOS isolates this from every other app.
  • It is not sent anywhere. There is no backend, no account system, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK compiled into the app.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data with it.

Export is something you do, deliberately

When you choose Export, the app writes a PDF or PNG and hands it to the iOS share sheet or print system. From that moment the destination is your choice. The app itself transmits nothing and learns nothing about where your export went.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calendar utility with no account system and no data collection, so it is suitable for all ages. It knowingly collects nothing about anyone, including children.

If a future version adds networking, analytics, or any form of collection, this page will be updated and its effective date changed before that version ships. The version of this policy that applies to you is the one published when you installed the version you are running.
